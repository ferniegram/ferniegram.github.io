Fernschreiber, Revamped

Ferniegram does not try to reinvent the wheel. Instead, it continues the development of Fernschreiber, combining the stability with the rich functionality of modern Telegram.

A modern current-day solution

Just like Telegram grew from a simple messaging application into a versatile platform for messaging, channels, bots and communities, Fernschreiber evolved from a basic Telegram client into Ferniegram - a refined application providing a modern messaging experience.
