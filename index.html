YAST Client

YAST Client is a yet another SailfishOS Telegram client.

Fernschreiber, Revamped

YAST Client does not try to reinvent the wheel. Instead, it continues the development of Fernschreiber, combining the stability with the rich functionality of modern Telegram.

A modern current-day solution

Just like Telegram grew from a simple messaging application into a versatile platform for messaging, channels, bots and communities, Fernschreiber evolved from a basic Telegram client into YAST Client - a refined application providing a modern messaging experience.
